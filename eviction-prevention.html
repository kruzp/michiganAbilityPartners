Line 04 · Eviction Prevention

Got a summons? You still have a stop before eviction.

Emergency rent assistance — up to $3,000 — for qualified Washtenaw County residents facing eviction for nonpayment of rent.

When rent falls behind, an eviction summons can feel like the end of the line. It doesn't have to be. MAP's Eviction Prevention Assistance helps qualified households in Washtenaw County catch up and stay put.

Assistance depends on eligibility and available funds, and processing times vary with how many applications we receive — so start as soon as you get your summons.

To apply you must

  • Live in Washtenaw County
  • Have received a court summons for eviction due to nonpayment of rent
  • Owe $3,000 or less in total — larger balances can't be approved

Questions

Before you apply.

I live outside Washtenaw County. Can I apply?

This fund is for Washtenaw County residents only. Call our Ann Arbor or Jackson office and we'll help you find the right next call — or dial 2-1-1 for local emergency rental help.

I owe more than $3,000. Should I still apply?

Applications with an outstanding balance over $3,000 are denied. Call us anyway — our staff may know of other options.

I'm behind on rent but haven't received a summons.

This program requires a court summons for nonpayment of rent. If you receive Social Security, SSI or VA benefits, our Representative Payee program can help you get ahead of it.

How long does it take?

Processing times vary with the number of applications and available funding. Apply as soon as you can.
