Line 01 · Housing

Homelessness should be rare, brief and one-time.

Permanent supportive housing with subsidized rent, a case manager in your corner, and help with the everyday skills that keep a home.

A stable home is hard to keep when you're also facing a disability, a mental health condition, substance use or a past conviction. MAP helps you find the right place, secure the money to get started, and keep it.

Staff meet one-on-one with each participant on daily living skills — budgeting, cooking, cleaning, hygiene and keeping appointments — through regular check-ins that fit your goals.

Permanent supportive housing is for people who are

  • Experiencing homelessness
  • Low-income
  • Living with a documented disability

Stops on this line

From referral to a home you keep.

  1. Connect with HAWC

    Housing Access for Washtenaw County is the front door. MAP receives its housing referrals from HAWC, so start there.

  2. Find the right place

    Your MAP team helps you find housing that suits you — including the 38 affordable homes MAP owns and operates at six sites.

  3. Get moved in

    We help secure the financial assistance to get started: deposits, first month, the basics.

  4. Build daily routines

    One-on-one coaching on budgeting, cooking, cleaning, hygiene and managing appointments.

  5. Stay housed

    Subsidized rent and long-term case management to break the cycle of chronic homelessness for good.
