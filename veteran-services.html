Line 03 · Veteran Services

You served. Now let's get you working.

The Homeless Veterans' Reintegration Program (HVRP) helps Veterans facing homelessness break down the barriers between them and a steady job.

HVRP is funded by the U.S. Department of Labor's Veterans' Employment and Training Service, and it's the only federal program focused entirely on employment for homeless Veterans. MAP has run it since 2015.

We match your strengths and interests to local openings, partner with employers who hire inclusively, and stay with you through the first weeks and months on the job.

You may be eligible if you

  • Served in the U.S. military and did not receive a dishonorable discharge
  • Are homeless or at risk of homelessness
  • Are ready to re-enter the workforce
  • Are working with HUD-VASH or SSVF, or live in transitional housing or a GPD program

From referral to steady work.

  1. Refer in

    Fill out the referral form or call either office. We'll set up a first conversation.

  2. Clear the barriers

    Career counseling and goal setting — and connections to housing and support services when you need them.

  3. Get job-ready

    Résumé support, interview practice, job skills and workplace readiness.

  4. Get placed

    We match you with employers who want Veterans on their team, and help you navigate hiring.

  5. Keep it

    Follow-up coaching on time management, communication and fitting in at a new job.
