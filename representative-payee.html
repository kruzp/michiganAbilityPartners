Line 02 · Representative Payee

Your benefits, working for you.

MAP is an SSA-approved representative payee. We receive your benefits, pay the essentials first, and help you build a budget that holds.

If budgeting or paying bills on time is a struggle — because of a disability, a mental health condition, or just a hard season — a representative payee can take the weight off. As your payee, MAP is required to act in your best interest at all times.

We started this program in 1989 and still offer the financial education and credit repair that came with it.

This line is for

  • People with disabilities
  • Senior citizens
  • People receiving Social Security, SSI or Veterans Administration benefits
  • Anyone at risk of losing housing, utilities or other essentials
  • People referred by a doctor, caseworker or the SSA

How the program works.

  1. You're matched with a payee

    One dedicated MAP representative payee who knows your situation.

  2. Bring your bills to the first meeting

    Copies of your essential bills — rent, utilities, anything recurring.

  3. Build a budget together

    We draft a preliminary budget with you, not for you.

  4. Essentials paid first

    Your SSA or SSI check comes to MAP. Rent, utilities and food are paid, then you receive your personal spending money.

  5. Adjust as life changes

    Regular meetings keep the budget current. Every dollar is recorded.
